Keeping Your San Francisco HVAC System Running Longer

Most mid-season breakdowns trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

How old is your system? Its age says a lot about what comes next. With regular service, most air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that point and is also showing higher bills, uneven temperatures, or frequent repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement in peak season.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet that same capacitor, neglected for a season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone today so you can reach us fast the moment something seems off in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.

Typical Heating & Cooling Issues in San Francisco

Most local HVAC failures give warning signs before they happen. A breaker that trips every few days, a condenser that hums louder each month, rooms drifting further from the thermostat setting, energy bills creeping up with nothing else changed. These are the early symptoms of failing capacitors, refrigerant leaks, dirty coils, and duct problems, and caught early they're an affordable repair. Ignored, they take out compressors and heat exchangers, the two most expensive parts in the system. We diagnose the cause, not just the symptom, so small problems stay small.
